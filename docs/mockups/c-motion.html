How pickup works

About a minute on your phone, zero minutes in line.

  1. Choose your drink

    Pick a size, milk and sugar. Your total updates as you go.

  2. Get your code

    A short code and QR ticket land on your phone. The bar starts brewing.

  3. Show it when it’s Ready

    The barista scans your ticket and your drink is yours.
