How pickup works

Three steps, about a minute on your phone.

  1. Choose your drink

    Pick a size, milk and sugar level. Your total updates as you go.

  2. Get your pickup code

    You receive a short code and a QR ticket. We start making your drink right away.

  3. Show it at the counter

    The barista scans your code and hands over your drink. No queue, no name shouting.

We hold every order for 30 minutes.

Why there’s a time limit

Your drink is made the moment you order. After 30 minutes it’s no longer the drink you paid for, so the order expires.

Missed pickups

An expired order counts as a missed pickup. Miss three and ordering pauses for 24 hours. You can still browse the menu while it’s paused.
